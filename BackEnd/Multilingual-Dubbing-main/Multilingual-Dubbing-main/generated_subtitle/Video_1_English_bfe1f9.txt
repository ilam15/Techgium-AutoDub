Do you know the difference between atheist and agnostic? Let's begin that, shall we? Technically, atheists are someone who doesn't believe in God. They say, nope, no God. While agnostics, on the other hand, they are a bit more on the fence. They neither confirm nor deny the God's existence. Well, now it is possible to be both an agnostic atheist, doesn't believe, but also doesn't think that he'll ever know what the God's existence is.
